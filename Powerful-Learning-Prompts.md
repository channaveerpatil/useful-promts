# ✨ Powerful Learning Prompts (Markdown Ready)

## 1. 🧠 Homework Completion Expert
**Prompt:**  
“Act as a top tutor and help me complete this homework step-by-step. Explain concepts simply, give examples, and teach the method rather than just the answer.  
Here is the assignment: **[insert homework]**.” 

---

## 2. 🧠 Step-by-Step Problem Solver
**Prompt:**  
“Solve this problem step-by-step and explain each step in simple terms so I can understand it. Focus on reasoning, not just the final answer.  
Problem: **[paste question]**  
Class/Level: **[your grade]**.”

---

## 3. 🧠 Exam Preparation Coach
**Prompt:**  
“Create a full study plan for **[subject]** based on my exam date **[date]** and available study time **[hours/day]**. Break down topics, daily tasks, revision plan, practice tests, and key scoring tips.”

---

## 4. 🧠 Past Paper & Question Bank Generator
**Prompt:**  
“Create practice questions and answers for **[topic]**, including MCQs, short responses, and long analytical questions similar to real exam papers.”

---

## 5. 🧠 Doubt & Concept Clarity Teacher
**Prompt:**  
“Explain this concept simply, like I’m 10, with examples. Then explain it again in a clear academic tone.  
Concept: **[insert concept]**.”

---

## 6. 🧠 Project / Presentation Creator
**Prompt:**  
“Create a project or presentation on **[topic]** with structure, key points, visuals, data examples, a conclusion, and a simple presentation script.”
